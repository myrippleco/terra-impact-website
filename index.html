Navigating a Changing Market

Sustainability is increasingly intertwined with business strategy, access to capital, energy security, infrastructure investment and long-term resilience.

At the same time, the landscape is becoming more complex. Regulatory approaches are diverging across markets. Investors are demanding greater financial and strategic rigor. AI and electrification are accelerating power and infrastructure needs. Climate impacts are changing the economics of assets and operations. Nature and biodiversity are emerging as financial considerations.

Terra Impact helps leaders determine what matters, where the opportunities lie and how capital can support their ambitions.

Meet Manju →

Manju Seal is Founder and Managing Partner of Terra Impact, where she advises boards, C-suite executives and senior leaders on sustainable finance, climate and sustainability strategy, capital markets and pathways to long-term value creation.

Her work brings together deep capital markets experience with an understanding of the evolving sustainability landscape. She works with corporations, financial institutions, investors, nonprofits and other mission-driven organizations seeking to translate climate, environmental and social priorities into credible strategies, financing approaches and investment opportunities.

Manju is particularly experienced in sustainable debt and capital mobilization, with expertise spanning green, social, sustainability, transition and sustainability-linked financing. Having worked across both the issuer and investor sides of the market, she brings a practical understanding of how sustainability objectives intersect with corporate strategy, financing decisions, investor expectations and market realities.

Previously, Manju was BMO Financial Group's inaugural Head of Sustainable Finance and Capital Markets ESG Lead, where she established and led the Sustainable Finance Advisory for corporate and institutional clients. She helped build sustainable finance as a new area of business within BMO Capital Markets and advised clients on sustainable bond underwriting, product development and other financing solutions.

She led the launch of BMO's Sustainable Bond Program and its landmark US$500 million sustainability bond issuance in 2019, co-authored BMO's Sustainable Financing Framework, and was a key contributor to the firm's CAD $400 billion sustainable finance commitment. She also served on BMO's firm-wide Sustainability Council.

Earlier in her career, Manju worked at Goldman Sachs in FICC Structured Finance and later led Fixed Income Risk & Performance Analytics at Goldman Sachs Asset Management. Her experience also includes fixed income portfolio management and quantitative investment analysis.

Beyond financial markets, Manju has more than two decades of leadership experience with nonprofits and social enterprises addressing environmental and social challenges. She is an American Marshall Fellow, Board President Emerita of Narika, former Executive Director of Singapore-based LEAP201, and a board director of Proximity Designs.

She holds master's degrees in business administration, mathematics and ethnomusicology.
